# YouTube PFP and banner

## Channel context

Use the user's channel profile and actual source asset. For Ship Sh!t Show, read [the bundled profile](references/channel-profile.md); viewers using another channel should provide their audience, voice, formats, links and visual references. A profile is context, not permission to publish, upload footage, buy generation or contact a partner.

Read supplied brand direction and inspect current assets/identity references. Resolve only missing consequential choices: represented person/show, exact name, existing logo, imagery rights and intended visual direction. Honor the latest approved direction instead of reopening a settled style.

Design the PFP and banner as related assets with different jobs. PFP: simple recognizable silhouette/mark or supplied portrait, legible in a tiny circular crop. Banner: responsive composition with exact text/logo inside the current safe area. Do not squeeze a full poster into the PFP. Do not invent hosts or transform their identity unless requested.

Provide a coordinated brief and complete prompts; generate when requested with the available image tool. Preserve supplied transparency for cutouts. Use an editable tool when layout/text precision requires it. For final exports, check exact spelling, face likeness, circular crop, desktop/mobile/TV banner crops, resolution and file size. Keep masters and exports separately with a manifest describing the references and generation/edit steps.

As of 2026-10-02, YouTube recommends a 2560×1440 banner, minimum 2048×1152, with 1235×338 text/logo safe area at that minimum size, and a 6 MB limit. A scaled guide at 2560 must be labelled as scaled, not quoted as YouTube's exact minimum guide. PFP displays at 98×98 with a 15 MB limit. Recheck [official specifications](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en) when exporting. A larger square master is a design choice, not a YouTube requirement.

Return assets/prompts plus crop previews and any unverified checks. Changing the channel's live branding requires publication authorization.
